# BIQS Academy Bahawalpur - AI Knowledge Base

## Overview
BIQS Academy is a premier educational coaching institute located in Bahawalpur, Punjab, Pakistan. Established in 2014, we have over 12 years of proven excellence in providing result-oriented academic coaching for Matriculation and Intermediate students, along with professional English Language training.

## Core Academic Programs

### Matriculation (9th & 10th Class)
- Science Group: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, English, Urdu, Islamiat, Pak Studies
- Computer Group: Computer Science, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, Urdu, Islamiat, Pak Studies
- Arts Group: Humanities with elective choices including Civics, Economics, Computer Science, Health & PE, Languages, Home Economics, Geography/History

### Intermediate (11th & 12th Class - FSc/ICS/FA)
- FSc Pre-Medical: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Urdu, Islamiat
- FSc Pre-Engineering: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, Urdu, Islamiat
- ICS (Computer Science): Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics/Statistics, English, Urdu
- FA (Humanities): Flexible combinations in Education, Civics, Economics, Sociology, Psychology, History, Geography, Fine Arts, Languages, and more

### English Language Course
- Professional spoken and written English training
- Grammar accuracy, vocabulary building, and fluency development
- Suitable for students, professionals, and general learners
